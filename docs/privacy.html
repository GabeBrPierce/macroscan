Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

MacroScan ("the app") is a calorie, macro and weight tracker for KaiOS. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: your personal tracking data stays on your device. We do not run servers, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect your diary, weight or food data.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter — your food diary, weight history, custom foods, goals and settings — is stored locally on your phone using the browser's local storage. If your device supports it, the app also writes a backup copy of this data to your sdcard so it survives reinstalling the app. This data is never sent to us.

Camera

The app can use your camera to scan food barcodes. Camera frames are processed on the device to read the barcode number. Images from the camera are not stored and not transmitted anywhere. Camera access is optional — you can also type a barcode or search for foods by name.

Network requests to nutrition databases

When you search for a food or look up a barcode, the app sends your search term or the barcode number to third-party nutrition databases to retrieve nutrition information:

  • USDA FoodData Central — fdc.nal.usda.gov
  • Open Food Facts — openfoodfacts.org

Only the search text or barcode is sent — no account, name or personal identifier. These requests are subject to those services' own privacy policies.

Advertising

MacroScan displays ads through KaiAds, the advertising network provided by KaiOS. KaiAds may collect data (such as device and usage information) to serve and measure ads, in accordance with its own privacy policy. See KaiAds Privacy Policy.

Children

MacroScan is a general-audience health app and is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can erase all of your data at any time from the app: open More → Clear all data. Uninstalling the app removes its local storage; if you enabled sdcard backups, you can delete the backup file (macroscan-backup.json) from your sdcard using the Files app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.
